# Use of AI tools

Software author and maintainer: Aymen Ochi. Scientific coauthor roles for Abdullah Muzil Alharbi are in AUTHORS.md.

ChatGPT and Codex (OpenAI) were used as assistive tools for troubleshooting technical problems, debugging and testing analysis code, checking consistency between numerical outputs and manuscript text, and supporting manuscript drafting and revision. Claude (Anthropic) was used for an additional internal computational audit, including a separate implementation of key calculations, suggestions for adjustment and calibration checks, and proposed manuscript revisions. Some post-audit test expectations were taken from calculations produced in that Claude-assisted audit and checked against the project implementation. These exercises simulated critical reviewer feedback during manuscript preparation; they were AI-assisted internal checks, not independent human peer review or journal review. No synthetic research observations were generated, and statistical figures were produced programmatically from computed results. The human authors retain responsibility for the research, verification of AI-assisted outputs, and final methods, interpretations and conclusions.
