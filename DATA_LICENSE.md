# OULAD data and derived-data licence

Source: Jakub Kuzilek, Martin Hlosta and Zdenek Zdrahal (2017), OULAD: Open University Learning Analytics Dataset, version 1. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.5081998.v1

Dataset article: https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2017.171

The original data are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The analysis software licence does not replace that licence. Retain attribution to the original creators and identify transformations when reusing the data.

Raw CSVs/archive are downloaded from the official versioned source and are not included in this GitHub package. data/raw/manifest.json and figshare_metadata.json record the pinned source bytes. Included processed cohorts, numerical output tables, student-level prediction/split records and data-derived figures originate from those public OULAD observations. They remain subject to the source-data attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms rather than being represented as newly collected observations under the MIT software licence.

Transformations: relational joins; recorded-date landmark eligibility; day-window positive-click activity/volume summaries; grouped residential-area IMD; fitted observational association and prediction analyses; generated tables and figures. The source student presentation years are 2013 and 2014. Later software, publication or verification dates do not create new cohorts.

No new participant identities or synthetic research observations were collected. IMD describes residential-area deprivation, not personal income. The licence is not an ethics determination for an author's institution.
